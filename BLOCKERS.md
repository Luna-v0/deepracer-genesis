Log a blocker instead of stalling, mark the subtask blocked in `PROJECT_MEMORY.md`, and
move to non-blocked work. Record the resolution here when it comes back.

```

- Blocked task/subtask:

- Question / decision needed:
- Default if unanswered:
- Status: OPEN | ASKED | RESOLVED
- Resolution:
```
